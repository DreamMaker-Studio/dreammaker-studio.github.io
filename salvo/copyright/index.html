Copyright & Takedown

Salvo by DreamMaker Studio · Effective date: 12 November 2026

We respect the rights of creators and rights holders. This page explains how Salvo works and how to tell us about a problem.

1. What Salvo does and does not do

  • Salvo does not host, store, index, catalogue or share any media. We run no library of content.
  • Files are fetched straight to the device of the person who started the download, from a link that person provided. They are not copied through our servers.
  • Salvo does not support YouTube, and does not download DRM-protected or paid streaming content.
  • Our terms require people to download only content they own, that is public domain or openly licensed, or that they have permission to save.

2. If you believe your work was copied without permission

Send a notice to dreammaker3d2026@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright notice". Please include:

  1. The link (URL) of the content you say is being copied or reached through the app, so we can identify and block it.
  2. A description of the work you own, and how you can show it (for example a link to the original, a registration, or other proof of ownership or authority to act for the owner).
  3. Your full name, your role (owner or authorised agent), and an email address or phone number where we can reach you.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the rights holder or are authorised to act for them.
  6. Your signature (typed name is fine).

Notices that are incomplete may take longer, because we may need to ask you for the missing details.

3. What we do

  • We review every notice we receive and aim to respond within 5 business days.
  • For a valid notice we block the reported source or link in the app. The block list is updated through app configuration or a new release.
  • We keep a record of notices and repeat reports about the same source.
  • Because files live only on users' devices, we cannot remove a file from someone's phone. Blocking the source stops the app from fetching it again.

4. Counter-notice

If a source was blocked by mistake, write to the same address with the subject "Counter-notice". Include the blocked link, why you believe the report was wrong or that you have permission, your name and contact details, and a statement that the information is accurate. We may pass a counter-notice to the person who made the report and restore access if they do not follow up, as the law and good practice allow.

5. If you saved something by mistake

You can delete any download from Library in the app at any time, or from your gallery.

6. Abuse of this process

Please send only accurate notices. Knowingly false claims can carry legal consequences.
