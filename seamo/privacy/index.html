Privacy Policy

Seamo by DreamMaker Studio · Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what Seamo ("the app") does and does not do with information. Seamo is published by DreamMaker Studio ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos, carousels and exports stay on your device. We do not upload, copy to a server or look at them.
  • Background removal runs on your device. Your photo is not sent anywhere.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, subject to your consent choices. Seamo Pro removes ads.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your photos, the carousels you make, their text, and the images or PDFs you export.
  • Your contacts, location, messages, or account credentials of other services.
  • Any account profile, because the app has no sign-in.

3. Photos you pick

You choose photos through Android's photo picker, so the app only gets the photos you select, not your whole library. To keep your projects working even if a photo is later moved or deleted, the app keeps a private copy of each selected photo inside its own storage on your device. Deleting a project deletes its copies. Exports are saved to Pictures/Seamo on your device when "Save to gallery" is on.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Buying Seamo Pro removes ads from the app.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: for ads, purchases and the one-time download of the background-removal model. Making and exporting carousels works offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.

The app does not request access to all your photos or files.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal account data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side.
  • To delete data on your device: delete projects in the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Exported images in Pictures/Seamo stay until you delete them.
  • Data held by Google services can be managed through your Google account's controls.

8. Children

Seamo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Brazil, California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we keep no identifiable data about you, most requests are answered by the controls in sections 5 and 7. You can write to us for anything else.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
