Privacy Policy

Salvo by DreamMaker Studio · Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what Salvo ("the app", "we") does and does not do with information. Salvo is published by DreamMaker Studio ("we", "us"). We wrote it to be short and readable.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The links you paste or share into Salvo are processed on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Files you download are saved on your device. We do not upload, copy or look at them.
  • Crash reports and basic usage statistics help us fix and improve the app. They never contain your links, video titles or file names, and you can switch each one off in Settings > Privacy.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob (unless you buy Premium), which may use your device's advertising ID, subject to your consent choices.

2. Information we do not collect

  • The content of your downloads, and the links (URLs), page titles or file names you open or download. They are not part of any crash report or usage statistic either.
  • Your contacts, location, photos, messages, or account credentials of other services.
  • Any account profile, because the app has no sign-in.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are separate from ads. You can switch each one off at any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • Buying Premium removes ads from the app.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to fetch the file you asked to download.
  • Notifications: to show download progress. You can decline and downloads still work.
  • Foreground service: to keep a download running while the app is in the background.

The app saves files to the shared Movies and Music folders through Android's standard media storage. It does not request access to all files on your device.

6. Where data stays

Your download history, settings and files are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes its settings and history; files saved to your Movies and Music folders stay until you delete them.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal account data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side.
  • To delete data held on your device: clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Delete downloaded files from the Library or your gallery.
  • Data held by Google services (ads, crash reports, analytics) can be managed through your Google account's activity controls. If you want us to ask a service to delete data tied to your installation, write to us and include your app version.

8. Children

Salvo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Brazil, California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not keep identifiable data about you, most requests are answered by the controls in section 4 and section 7. You can write to us for anything else.

10. Security

Links and files stay on your device, which reduces what could ever be exposed. Network requests made by the app use HTTPS where the source supports it.

11. International transfers

Google and Firebase may process data in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards. We do not run servers that store your data.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
